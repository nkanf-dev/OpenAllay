Source inventory scope and limitations

SOURCES.json pins the stock sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 engine source archive, official JVM
and native jars, eSpeak/Piper source archives, and per-platform ORT vendor bundles.
UPSTREAM-DEPENDENCY-SOURCE-DECLARATIONS.json retains source URL/hash declarations
read from the matching engine archive's CMake files. These declarations are not
a linked binary inventory. Optional dependency declarations do not prove linkage.

The ASR-DEPENDENCY-* license copies were inspected in the independently built
macOS ARM64 ASR-only package. They identify known source/build dependencies and
retain their full license texts. Their presence here does not claim that every
stock CPU jar links every listed component, or that optional stock components
have all been inventoried.

GPL3.txt applies to eSpeak NG contained in official stock JNI. The exact eSpeak
and Piper dependency revisions are pinned in SOURCES.json. The engine's source
archive includes build recipes and its dependency scripts. The source files and
metadata are source references, not a written source offer or certification of
complete corresponding source. Applicable redistribution obligations must be
satisfied under the actual distribution arrangement.

The macOS ARM64 ORT vendor bundle's GIT_COMMIT_ID was inspected and is recorded.
Other five platform vendor source commit identities were not read from their
bundles. Their declared version and binary archive hashes are retained without
promoting one platform's source fact to all six. No general all-platform source
reproduction or complete-license-inventory claim is made by this collection.
